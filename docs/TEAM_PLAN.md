## 5. Presentation plan

Planned for a 20-minute slot followed by questions: about 17 minutes of slides plus about 3 minutes
of live demo. The slide numbers and speakers match the deck (`docs/presentation/BedSynth_AI.pptx`,
one PowerPoint section per part), whose speaker notes carry the same target times; scale the times
to the slot you are given.

| # | Part | Speaker | Slides | Time | Content | Show |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Problem, scope and architecture | A | 1-4 | 2 min | why hospitals need synthetic, non-clinical operations data; why synthetic data; the pipeline | README overview; DESIGN §2 diagram |
| 2 | Data preparation and scenarios | A | 5-7 | 3 min | the two tables of the simulated reference hospital, injected data-quality issues, cleaning and repair, hard and soft rules, SDV-ready dataset; the 26 scenarios mapped to the brief's families with their support | Week 1 report §1.2, §3-§6, §9; `reports/figures/week1_*.png` |
| 3 | SDV pipeline | B | 8-10 | 4 min | the five experiments (metadata, segmentation, marginal selection, constraint groups) and why two single-table models rather than SDV's HMA or PAR, how a programmable constraint works, the generator (conditions, filters, rejection sampling, allocation strata, calendar mode) | Week 2 report §3, §5; DESIGN §1.1; `reports/figures/week2_raw_validity.png` |
| 4 | Evaluation | C | 11-16 | 4 min | composite components, real-data ceiling and no-learning floor, composite results, improvements (ablation and the band-grouped A/B test), occupancy and scheduling patterns, scenario coverage, privacy and utility | Week 3 report §2-§3, §5-§7; `reports/figures/week3_*.png` |
| 5 | API, testing and live demo | D | 17-18, then the demo | 2 min + 3 min demo | endpoints, one request and its response metadata, testing evidence (fast and slow suites, API demo); then the short demo of `docs/DEMO_SCRIPT.md` section 4 | `/ui`; Week 4 report §1-§3, §5 |
| 6 | Limitations and next steps | C, then all | 19-20 | 2 min | simulated reference data, independent tables, no day-to-day census continuity and what that means for optimisation testing, measured gaps, future work; closing | README Limitations; DESIGN §13 |
| 7 | Questions | All | - | rest | first answerer per topic below | - |

Live demo (D, 3 minutes): follow the short version in `docs/DEMO_SCRIPT.md` section 4 - the ready
pill of `/ui`, a high-occupancy ward_daily request with seed 42 and "Evaluate quality" ticked (tiles,
Validation, Quality), `/validate` catching a broken record, an impossible filter refused before
sampling, and a saved dataset downloaded as CSV. Start `python scripts/04_serve_api.py` before the
talk and open `/ui` (it works offline; `/docs` needs internet for its assets); the pill reads
*Starting · loading the engine…* for the first seconds and then *Ready*. Keep requests small. The
presenting machine needs the trained `models/`, plus `data/processed/` for quality scores and
scenario support; if the service fails, use the fallback plan of `docs/DEMO_SCRIPT.md` section 5
(`reports/api_demo/summary.md` and the saved JSON of the same calls).
